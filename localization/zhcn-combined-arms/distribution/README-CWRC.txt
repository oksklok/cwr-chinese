CWRC — 免费社区汉化 / Free community Chinese translation

适用于 / Requires Windows x64 Cold War Assault Remastered 3.05.

1. 解压到 Remastered 目录（原版 PoseidonGame.exe 所在目录）。
   Extract beside the original PoseidonGame.exe. Do not overwrite stock files.
2. 双击 CWRC.cmd，直接开始游戏。无需安装或准备。
   Run CWRC.cmd. The mod is ready to play: no setup or preparation.
3. 在 Options > Game > Text language 选择简体中文或繁體中文。
   Select Simplified or Traditional Chinese in Options > Game > Text language.
   游戏会保存选择；其他已选模组保留；配音使用英语。
   Your language choice is saved. Other selected mods remain enabled; voices
   use English. English/French text remains available in the same menu.
4. 移除：关闭游戏，删除 @CWRC、CWRC.cmd 和 README-CWRC.txt。
   Remove: close the game and delete @CWRC, CWRC.cmd and README-CWRC.txt.
   Profiles/saves are managed by the game and are not deleted.

The original executable remains untouched. The included modified localization
client is needed for Chinese language selection, SC/TC font switching and
campaign text overrides. The stock executable alone is insufficient.
Conflicting total-conversion/config mods may be incompatible. No support is
claimed for original 1.96/1.99 or future updates. Microsoft x64 Visual C++ 2022
runtime is required if not already installed.
Remove an older experimental CWRC installer with its own uninstaller first.
When upgrading from the preparation-based ZIP, delete its three CWRC entries
before extracting this ZIP; keep profiles and saves.
